# Why Naga Codex Cyber Security

The market has excellent **libraries** (hundreds of skills) and excellent **narrow packs**
(code review only, AI-only, offensive-only). Consulting delivery needs something else:
a **curated operating system** for real engagements.

| Capability | Mega libraries (800+ skills) | Code-only packs | AI-only packs | **Naga Codex** |
|------------|------------------------------|-----------------|---------------|----------------|
| AppSec depth | Broad | Strong | Light | Strong |
| AI / MCP / Agentic 2026 | Partial | Rare | Strong | Strong |
| EU/DE compliance (GDPR, NIS2, BSI) | Weak | Rare | Rare | **Core** |
| Client report EN+DE | Rare | Rare | Rare | **Core** |
| Authorization ethics gate | Varies | Varies | Varies | **Mandatory** |
| Normalized finding schema | Some | Some | Rare | **Yes** |
| Engagement orchestration | DIY | DIY | DIY | **`full-security-audit`** |
| Agent noise / discovery cost | High | Low | Low | **Low (25 skills)** |
| Brand / commercial fit | Generic | Generic | Generic | **Naga Codex** |

## Design principles
1. **Quality over quantity** — 25 production skills beat 800 unread ones
2. **Framework honesty** — real IDs only; never invent controls
3. **Consulting workflow** — gate → assess → report → roadmap
4. **DACH-ready** — bilingual templates, NIS2/BSI/GDPR spine
5. **Portable** — agentskills.io standard; Claude, Cursor, Hyperagent, others
6. **Defensive by default** — authorized testing; no weapon dump
